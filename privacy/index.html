Privacy policy

Effective October 24, 2026

Rewarded is a family money and points tracker maintained by MrSeizy. This policy covers the Rewarded app, its extensions, and this support website.

Your family’s records are stored on your devices and synced using Apple’s iCloud. Rewarded does not send those records to a server operated by the developer. The app has no advertising, analytics SDKs, or tracking. You can choose to share information with family members or through other apps.

What the app stores

Rewarded stores the information your family enters and the information needed to display it:

  • Kids’ names and avatars, including photos a parent chooses.
  • Money or points transactions, dates, notes, reasons, and the parent name associated with an entry.
  • Goals and their pictures, target amounts, saved allocations, and completion status.
  • Behaviors, custom quotes, praise, job offers, and recurring allowance settings.
  • Device preferences, such as the selected kid, appearance, notification settings, and which device posts an allowance.

Balances, progress, recaps, and achievements are calculated from this information. Rewarded does not process payments or connect to bank accounts.

Where information is kept

Family records are saved locally and synced through CloudKit in the family owner’s iCloud account. Invited participants receive the shared records through their own iCloud accounts. The developer does not receive access to your private or shared family database through the app.

Device appearance preferences and pictures chosen in “My style” are kept on that device rather than synced as family records. Device data may also be included in backups according to your Apple settings. Apple operates iCloud under its privacy policy.

Photos

The app uses Apple’s system photo picker and receives only the photos you select, rather than access to your entire photo library. Parent-selected kid avatars and goal photos are stored with family records. Device-specific appearance photos stay with that device’s settings. Photos attached to a request are shared only when you choose to send or share the request.

Sharing with your family

The family owner manages invitations using Apple’s iCloud sharing interface in Settings → General → Family Sharing. “Can make changes” gives another parent editing access; “View only” gives a kid’s device read-only access. A kid’s view displays the selected kid’s information. Family records are shared with the participants the owner invites, not published in a public database.

The owner can remove participants or stop sharing through that same interface. Removing access does not erase copies someone previously exported, saved, or sent elsewhere.

Exports and requests

A parent can create a CSV export containing names, history, amounts, praise, and notes, then choose a destination in the system share sheet. Rewarded creates a temporary file on the device; iOS manages temporary storage. Copies saved or shared elsewhere are controlled by the destination you choose.

“Ask a parent” prepares a message, optionally with a photo, in Messages or the system share sheet. You choose the recipient or app and confirm sending. Sending a request does not change the family’s records. The selected messaging or sharing service handles the information under its own policies.

Safari goal imports

When you share a page to “Add to Rewarded,” the extension uses the page information or share items supplied by Safari or another app. A draft can contain the page URL, product name, price, currency, and an available image. The extension does not independently fetch the webpage.

Drafts are kept locally for a parent to review. Saving or dismissing a draft removes it from the inbox. Drafts older than seven days are discarded when the inbox is next checked. Confirming a goal saves its name, amount, and chosen photo with the family’s records.

Notifications, widgets, and Shortcuts

Notifications and reminders are created on the device using the records available there. Apple’s background services support iCloud updates. Notification content is not sent through a developer-operated server.

Widgets read a local copy of the names, balances, avatars, and goals they display. Shortcuts and controls can open a reward form for a parent to review and save. These features do not add an analytics or advertising service.

Your choices and deleting data

  • Edit or delete records: use the parent controls in Rewarded. Deleting a kid in Settings → General → Kids also removes that kid’s transactions, behaviors, goals, praise, jobs, and allowance schedules. Changes sync to participants when their devices connect.
  • Manage sharing: the family owner can remove participants or stop sharing in Settings → General → Family Sharing.
  • Manage notifications: use Rewarded’s notification settings or iOS Settings → Notifications → Rewarded. You can use the app without allowing notifications.
  • Remove app and cloud data: deleting the app removes its local app data but does not by itself delete the family’s iCloud records. The family owner can manage Rewarded’s cloud data in iOS Settings → their name → iCloud → Storage or Manage Account Storage. Available labels vary by OS version.

Family records remain on the relevant devices and in iCloud until you delete them. Backups, exported files, sent messages, and recipients’ saved copies must be managed separately through the services that hold them. The developer cannot retrieve or delete your private iCloud family records for you.

Children

Rewarded is managed by parents, who decide what information to enter and which devices to invite. Kid devices have read-only access to family records and can prepare requests for a parent. The app does not transmit children’s family records to the developer, run advertising, or track children across apps or websites. A parent or guardian should handle support requests.

This website and support

This website uses no analytics scripts, advertising, embedded third-party content, or cookies set by us. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, as explained in GitHub’s Pages documentation and GitHub’s privacy statement.

If you open a support issue on GitHub, your GitHub username and anything you post are visible to the developer and the public. We use the information you choose to provide to respond and troubleshoot. Issue content remains on GitHub unless edited or removed; closing an issue does not delete it. GitHub’s own retention rules also apply.

Please use generic examples and do not post children’s names, family photos, balances, private notes, Apple Account details, or other sensitive information. You can request removal of an issue you opened without repeating the sensitive content.

Changes to this policy

We will publish updates on this page and change the effective date when the policy changes.

Contact

For privacy questions or help using Rewarded, visit our support page or open a support issue. A GitHub account is required to post. Keep requests general and omit personal family information.
